GhostChat is an anonymous, end-to-end encrypted, ephemeral chat.

The Android phone itself becomes the server: the app runs a local HTTP/WebSocket server, exposes it to the internet via Cloudflare Quick Tunnel, and generates an E2EE passphrase and 3 invite codes. Participants open the link in any browser, enter the passphrase, and chat.

When the app is closed, the session vanishes: no messages stored, no logs, no accounts.

Features:
- End-to-end encryption (AES-GCM 256, PBKDF2 250k, ECDSA P-256)
- Forward secrecy per message (HKDF)
- Multi-language UI (IT/EN/ES)
- Foreground Service for background survival
- FLAG_SECURE (blocks screenshots)

Technical note: Cloudflared (the official Go client) is broken on Android without root. GhostChat uses a forked pure-Rust QUIC client with JNI bindings and a custom DoH resolver to bypass OEM DNS filtering. No root, no Termux, no VPS.
